## Introduction

BRAWLVERSE sets a small number of cookies. We use them to keep you signed in and to remember your language choice. We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party profiling.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored by your browser when you visit a site. BRAWLVERSE uses two of them, plus a server-side session record.

## The cookies we set

### Session cookie

- **Name**: `better-auth.session_token` (and related auth cookies)
- **Purpose**: Keeps you signed in and protects the sign-in form. Required for the site to work.
- **Lifetime**: Until you sign out or the session expires.

### Locale cookie

- **Name**: `NEXT_LOCALE`
- **Purpose**: Remembers whether you chose English or Chinese so we render the right language next time.
- **Lifetime**: Until you clear it.

### Server-side session record

Not a browser cookie, but the matching server record that lets us validate your session on every request. It stores your user id and session metadata.

## Third-party requests

Two features can load third-party scripts, and only when you interact with them:

- **Cloudflare Turnstile** — if human verification is enabled, it sets a cookie to detect bots before a vote or a danmaku is accepted.
- **Google / GitHub sign-in** — used only when you choose to sign in with one of them. Their own privacy policies apply to those flows.

## Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking the session cookie means you will not be able to sign in; blocking the locale cookie just means the language resets to the default.

## Changes

If we change which cookies we set, we will update this page.
